# bug-circus-statusline

Turn debugging into a circus performance! Watch performers juggle bugs while the audience reacts to your coding show with dynamic applause and reactions. Displays: Show number (incremental counter), Rotating performers (🤹 juggler, 🎭 drama, 🎪 circus, 🎨 artist, 🎯 target - cycles every 5 shows), Random audience reactions (👏 applause 30% chance, 😴 sleeping 70% chance for each of 3 audience members).
