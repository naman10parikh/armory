## What it is
Multi-language crypto news for AI assistants — editorial summaries with sentiment labels (BULLISH/NEUTRAL/BEARISH) and importance scores (0–100). 6 tools across 8 languages, every story credits the original publisher. Install: `uvx zipp-mcp` or `ghcr.io/deficlow/zipp-mcp`. Public Streamable HTTP endpoint at `https://zippfeed.com/mcp/`; listed on the [Official MCP Registry](https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io/v0/servers?search=zipp).
