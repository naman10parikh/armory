## What it is
Lets an AI share the same live browser tab as a person, receiving the user's Alt+click target and enabling the AI to inspect, click, fill, type, navigate, or otherwise operate on that real page. It runs locally and pairs with the browser extension to bridge the user's page to the MCP client.
