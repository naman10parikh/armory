> `armory-mcp` server, and the plugins). Not a cataloged component. Persona → [SOUL.md](./SOUL.md).

## Name

**Armory** — the room where an army keeps its weapons, maintained and ready for issue. The agent
persona that keeps it is **Armiger** (the arms-bearer — see SOUL.md). The name was chosen over
earlier candidates (Engram, Pantheon) because it says exactly what the product does: agents walk
in, gear up, walk out armed.

## Symbolic connection to the workflow

Every harness component is a piece of **gear**: an MCP server is a power tool, a skill is a
technique manual, a hook is a tripwire, a sub-agent is a squire. Hand-rolling one is forging your
own sword the night before battle. The Armory already has 28,000 of them — racked
(12 category folders), labeled (provenance frontmatter), cross-referenced (`related:` edges in the
brain), and quality-checked before racking (the Hamel test-gate). `armory install` is the
quartermaster handing it over the counter, fitted to whatever harness you walked in wearing
(Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, OpenCode, Gemini).
